What to expect

The shape of the program

  • Build & drive. Assemble the chassis — steering servo, motor, camera, Raspberry Pi — then drive it by wire from Python.
  • Classical autopilot. An OpenCV lane detector and a steering controller that students tune until the car laps the track on its own.
  • Train the driver. Behavioral cloning: your best laps become training data for a neural network that steers from camera alone — the same loop used on real autonomous vehicles.
  • Grand Prix finale. Autonomous time trials and head-to-head races, scored live for families.
