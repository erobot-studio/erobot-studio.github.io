What every LeKiwi program shares

Research-grade, open source

LeKiwi comes from the open-source LeRobot ecosystem (Hugging Face) and is used by university labs and researchers worldwide. Students can look up everything they build.

The full stack in one machine

Arm + wheels + cameras means manipulation, navigation, perception, and AI all live on one robot — no toy abstractions, no black boxes.

Kid-safe by design

Low-voltage (5–12 V) electronics throughout, desk-scale size and speeds, taped driving arenas, and battery discipline taught from day one.
