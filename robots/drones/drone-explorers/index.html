What to expect

The shape of the program

  • Safety first, always. Indoor arenas, propeller guards, and pilot discipline from minute one.
  • Block-coded missions. Takeoff → square → land, then obstacle courses with loops and sensors.
  • Robot senses. Games that teach what sensors do — and what happens without them.
  • Mission showcase. A team flying mission performed for families on the final day.
