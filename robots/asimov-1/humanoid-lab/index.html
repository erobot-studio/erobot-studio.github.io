What to expect

The shape of the program

  • Sim-first, like real labs. Every pair gets a simulation workstation where crashing is free; time on the physical robot is earned with simulation results.
  • Balance & walking. Center-of-mass experiments, gait tuning in simulation, and supervised walking on the real robot — harness on.
  • Whole-body AI. Perception, manipulation while balancing, and an imitation-learning stretch goal on the LeRobot stack.
  • Humanoid Showcase. An interactive live routine for families — greet a visitor, fetch and deliver on request.
