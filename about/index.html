About eRobot Studio

A research lab, scaled down to kid size.

eRobot Studio exists because the tools of modern robotics — real arms, real AI training, real open-source stacks — are finally cheap enough to put in kids' hands. We refuse to water them down.

Teaching philosophy

Every session ends with the robot doing something new.

All programs share one spine — build it, program it, teach it — and one weekly rhythm. No lectures that outlast attention; the concept lesson is exactly as long as the lab needs.

MinutesBlock
0–10Hook & recap — the robot does something new, or last week's cliffhanger resolves
10–25Concept mini-lesson — the one idea this week's lab needs
25–75Hands-on lab — students in pairs at robot stations, instructor coaching
75–85Team challenge — every pair demos the week's new skill
85–90Cleanup, battery charging, next-week teaser
Safety & policies

Built for parents' peace of mind

  • Low-voltage everything. All robots run on 5–12 V — the same safety class as toys and laptops. Battery and tool discipline are taught as engineering habits.
  • Controlled arenas. Robots drive inside taped arenas; drones fly indoors with propeller guards and netting.
  • Safeguarding. Background-checked instructors, never one adult alone with one child, signed waivers, and photo/publication consent — nothing goes online without your sign-off.
  • Make-up sessions. With at most 4 students per cohort, missed weeks get a catch-up before the next session.
